Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026   Last reviewed: 13 November 2026

This policy describes personal information Zimshop AI handles to operate product discovery, seller accounts, business dashboards, messaging integrations and subscription/payment features. It should be read with our Terms and Conditions.

Marketplace notice: A seller's business name, contact number, shop location, delivery information and published listing are shown to people using Smart Match. Do not submit personal or confidential information in a public listing.

1. Who we are and contact

Zimshop AI operates in Zimbabwe as a product discovery and business connection platform. For privacy questions or requests, contact us on 0782029738 or through our Contact Us page. Please identify the account or information concerned and the request you wish to make. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify your identity and protect other people.

2. Information we handle

Depending on how you use the platform, information may include:

  • Seller account details such as account email, business name and password hash (the password itself is not stored as plain text).
  • Seller profile and public listing details, including business phone number, location, delivery information, product descriptions, prices, condition, stock status and photos.
  • Search terms, filters and product preferences entered into Smart Match.
  • Business-managed customer contact details, interests, consent flags, campaign and message information, and SMS conversation content.
  • Subscription email, plan, payment reference, payment status and related billing records. Payment credentials are handled by the relevant payment provider, not collected as card details by Zimshop AI.
  • Technical information necessary to operate the service, including session identifiers and service/security records generated by the platform or its providers.

3. How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts; publish seller profiles and listings; search and rank relevant listed products; facilitate communications that a customer requests; operate business dashboards and SMS tools; record subscription and payment status; respond to support, privacy and complaint requests; protect the platform; and meet legal obligations.

Search queries may be sent to a configured AI service to identify product search terms or assist with business workflows. When AI services are not configured or available, the platform may use its built-in matching and response logic.

4. Sharing and service providers

When a customer chooses to contact a business, the customer may share their message and contact details directly with that business through their own SMS or other communication application. Seller contact and listing details are publicly displayed so customers can find and contact the business.

Platform data may be processed by hosting/database providers and, where configured and needed, AI providers such as OpenAI, Groq, Cloudflare Workers AI or Google Gemini; SMS gateway providers; and payment providers such as Paynow. Each provider processes information under its own terms and technical practices. Depending on provider configuration and infrastructure, information may be processed outside Zimbabwe.

We do not sell personal information. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect users and platform security, or in connection with a lawful business reorganisation.

5. Messaging, consent and marketing

Businesses are responsible for having an appropriate basis and consent before using contact information for SMS or marketing. The platform records service and marketing consent separately where those features are used. Promotional communications should stop when a recipient opts out. Do not upload contact lists or send unsolicited marketing.

When you use a link that opens your SMS or messaging application, the draft is prepared on your device. You choose whether to send it. Your carrier or messaging provider may process the message and charge you.

6. Cookies, sessions and device storage

Seller sign-in uses a secure session cookie to keep the account signed in. The subscription workspace may store a billing email and payment reference in browser local storage to restore billing status on that device. The landing page stores your cookie preference selections in browser local storage; this preference storage is necessary to remember your choices. Analytics and advertising cookies are not currently used on the landing page. The public landing page does not require an account to search listings. Your browser and hosting/network providers may also process technical request information.

7. Retention

Smart Match stores the product search phrase and number of results without customer contact details. The platform retains these search records for the period configured in the business workspace: 7, 30, 60, 90, 180 or 365 days. If the business disables automatic expiry, search records are not automatically deleted. Expired search records are removed at application startup, during subsequent searches and by a daily cleanup task. This search-record schedule does not apply to customer contacts, SMS conversation history, product listings or subscription records. Those records do not all currently expire automatically; contact us to request review of information associated with you. Deletion may be limited where information must be retained for security, dispute resolution or legal obligations.

8. Security

We use measures intended to protect information, including password hashing and protected account sessions. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Users should use unique passwords, protect their devices and report suspected account or data security problems promptly through our contact number.

9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable Zimbabwean law, you may ask to be informed about processing, request access to personal information, request correction, object to certain processing, request deletion of inaccurate or misleading information, or withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing. Contact us at 0782029738. We may need to verify your identity and explain any legal or operational reason a request cannot be fully met.

You can stop marketing messages using the available opt-out instructions. Clearing browser local storage removes billing email/reference convenience values stored on that device; signing out clears the seller session cookie. Neither action deletes server records.

10. Children

The platform is not designed to collect information directly from children without appropriate involvement of a parent or guardian. Users under 18 should use the service with appropriate parental or guardian involvement, as described in our Terms.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform or applicable requirements change. The latest review and effective dates will be shown on this page. Material updates may also be communicated through the platform.

12. Applicable law

Personal information is handled subject to applicable Zimbabwean requirements, including the Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07]. See also our Terms and Conditions for consumer, marketplace and account terms.
